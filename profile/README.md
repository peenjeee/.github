## What Aidnara Means

**Aidnara AI** means **human-centered aid with AI-verified impact**.

- **Aid** represents help, donation, and social support.
- **Nara** gives the brand a people-first identity, focused on beneficiaries and real stories.
- **AI** represents automated proof analysis, impact summaries, and certificate wording.

## Mission

Make donations easier to trust by connecting on-chain transparency, proof-based reporting, and verifiable impact certificates.

## Core Product

Aidnara AI helps organizers and donors complete one transparent flow:

1. Create a donation campaign.
2. Donate with wallet on BNB Smart Chain.
3. Record donations on-chain.
4. Upload proof of fund usage.
5. Generate AI-assisted impact reports.
6. Issue donor and organizer certificates.
7. Verify certificates through QR/hash records on-chain.

## Key Features

- Transparent donation campaign dashboard
- BNB Smart Chain transaction tracking
- Proof upload and hash registry
- AI impact report generation
- Trust score and risk summary
- Donor certificate of impact
- Organizer transparency certificate
- QR-based public verification
